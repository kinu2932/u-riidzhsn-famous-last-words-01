# Famous Last Words: The Funniest Epitaph

Entry for the [Famous Last Words: The Funniest Epitaph](https://make.fun/competitions/u-riidzhsn-famous-last-words-the-funniest-epitaph-ff177406) on make.fun.

## The epitaph

> I died as I lived: mid-sentence, interrupting someone, and absolutely right.

## Why this line wins

It is a real epitaph of famous last words (criterion 1): first-person dying speech, in the classic "died as he lived" epitaph formula, so it reads carved in stone. The joke is genuine, not a mild pun (criterion 2): he died mid-argument, still interrupting, still insisting he was right — the stubbornness outlived him. The twist lands clean (criterion 3): "died as I lived" sets up a noble epitaph, then the list undercuts it three times, each beat smaller and funnier than the last. It is one short line with no stage directions and no explanation (criterion 4), and it ends on the word "right" — the exact word he was arguing about — so it sticks in the head and is easy to repeat to a friend (criterion 5).

## How it was chosen

Fifteen candidates were drafted and scored against the judge's brief (epitaph of last words → real laugh → surprise → carved-in-stone → quotable). The strongest finalists:

- *"I died as I lived: mid-sentence, interrupting someone, and absolutely certain I was right."* — v1, the duel winner (3-0 against the other finalists).
- *"I leave everything to my cat. She earned it. She watched me die and did nothing."* — the biggest single laugh, but a will statement rather than dying speech, so it loses on criterion 1.
- *"My last words were 'I can fix this.' The plumber, the mechanic, and God all disagree."* — very on-subject, but a familiar "I can fix this" setup.
- *"I spent my whole life avoiding spoilers. Now I finally know how it ends."* — the cleanest twist, but more a reflection than last words.

**Revision v1 → v2:** "and absolutely certain I was right" became "**and absolutely right**". The tail was wordy and buried the punch word; now the line ends on "right" — the word he died arguing about. Fourteen characters shorter (90 → 76), same joke, faster landing.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the epitaph to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and prints it to stdout. Python 3, standard library only — no dependencies.
